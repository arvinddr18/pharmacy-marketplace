Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Arnora (“we”, “our”, “us”) operates a medicine-delivery platform that connects customers with licensed partner pharmacies for home delivery of medicines and health products. We are starting in Tarikere, Karnataka, and expanding to more cities across Karnataka and India. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using the Arnora app or website, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and phone number.
  • Delivery details: your delivery address and your device’s GPS location, used to deliver your order and to calculate the delivery distance and fee.
  • Order details: the medicines and items you order, any prescription images you upload, and your order history.
  • Payment: payment is collected in person at delivery (cash or UPI). We do not collect or store your card or bank details.
  • Technical data: basic device and usage information, and notification tokens if you enable notifications.

How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Send your order to a partner pharmacy, and arrange delivery to you.
  • Calculate the delivery fee based on distance.
  • Send order updates and important notices by email or notification.
  • Provide customer support and improve our service.
  • Meet legal, tax and regulatory requirements.

Prescriptions

For medicines that legally require a prescription, you upload a prescription image. This is shared only with the licensed partner pharmacy fulfilling your order, which is responsible for verifying it and dispensing the medicine in line with the law.

Who we share information with

  • Partner pharmacies — to prepare and dispense your order.
  • Delivery partners (riders) — your name, address, phone and location, only to deliver your order.
  • Service providers — such as our email and hosting providers, who process data on our behalf.
  • Authorities — where required by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data security & retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We keep your data while your account is active and for as long as needed for legal and operational purposes.

Your rights

In line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may access, correct or request deletion of your personal data. You can deactivate or delete your account from the app’s Account settings, or contact us using the details below.

Children

Arnora is intended for use by adults (18 years or older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with its updated date.
